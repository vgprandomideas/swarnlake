Payment Flow

How entry payment works

  1. Select the events you want to join.
  2. Tap the UPI / GPay button with your total amount.
  3. Mention your flat number in the payment remarks.
  4. Upload the screenshot or PDF receipt before submitting.

A full online checkout provider can be enabled later when committee credentials are available. The current live flow is UPI / GPay with proof upload.
